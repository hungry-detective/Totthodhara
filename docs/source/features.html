What it does

Your second clipboard, always one click away

Everything below is the real app, rendered by Totthodhara itself.

Click to paste

Text, links, colors, images and files land on the shelf as cards. Click one and it's pasted into the app you're using. Shift+click several, then Paste All.

Drop into chat, mail, Explorer…

Drag cards into any app

Drag an image or file card straight into WhatsApp, a browser upload, email or Explorer — it arrives as the real file with its original name.

Pins & snippets

Pin what you need today, save what you need forever. Pinned cards stay at the front; snippets are never auto-cleaned.

Live meters & world clock

CPU, RAM, real network speed (physical adapters only — no VPN double counting) and time zones. Drag meters to rearrange them.

Follows your theme

System, Dark or Light — live with Windows. Three bar sizes where text and icons scale and stay centered, plus card styles and tones.

Shelf hidden

Stays out of the way

Docks above the taskbar without covering windows, and hides itself in fullscreen video and games — even while you seek.
